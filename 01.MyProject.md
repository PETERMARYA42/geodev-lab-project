# Week 01. My project brief

## The question

>Which rural settlements in the Chamwino district are disconnected from the school catchment zones?

---

## Why it matters

>Because in rural parts of Tanzania, living far from a school stops children from getting an education. When the walk to school is too long or dangerous, many children especially girls are forced to drop out. By showing the real-world problems students face, it helps the government spend money wisely to build schools or dormitories in the perfect spots where they will help the most children.

---

## The data I need

- District boundaries
- Schools
- Population
- DEM
- Transport networks
