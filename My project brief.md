# My project brief

## The question
Which rural settlements in the Chamwino district are disconnected from the secondary school catchment zones?

## Why it matters
Because in rural parts of Tanzania, living far from a school stops children from getting an education. When the walk to school is too long or dangerous, many children especially girls are forced to drop out. By showing the real-world problems students face, it helps the government spend money wisely to build schools or dormitories in the perfect spots where they will help the most children.

## The data I need
- District boundaries
- Schools
- Population
- DEM
- Transport networks

## Data sources required
- District boundaries - geoBoundaries - https://www.geoboundaries.org - GeoJSON - 5 MB
- Schools - OSM via HDX - https://data.humdata.org - GeoPackage 6.4 MB
- Population - WorldPop - https://www.worldpop.org - 407 MB
- DEM - OpenTopology - https://www.opentopography.org - 83.7 MB
- Transport networks - Geofabrik - https://www.geofabrik.de

## What I will build
I will build a primary output of the analysis is a spatially optimized rural school allocation master plan, which utilizes spatial data to identify educational access gaps and calculate precise infrastructure locations.
